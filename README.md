# Rolling Head

## About the game

Rolling Head is a quirky 3D arcade romp: you are a rolling head on a mission,
barrelling through colorful levels to knock down every target in your path.
Aim your rolls, build momentum and bounce off walls to line up the perfect
strike — every stage is a bite-sized physics playground.

Collect coins as you go and spend them on new looks for your unstoppable
noggin. Short levels, quick retries and rising chaos make it an easy game to
pick up and a hard one to put down.

## Controls

### Smartphone & tablet

- Drag to aim, release to launch the head.
- On-screen buttons to switch skins or retry a level.

### Desktop

- Click and drag to aim and launch.
- Roll into every target to clear the stage.
